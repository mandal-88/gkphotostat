About G.K. Photostat

Bulk Photocopying & Paper Books for Legal Work

Our Story

Specialists in Legal Photocopying

G.K. Photostat specialises in bulk photocopying for legal purposes. From our shop inside the Delhi High Court (LCB-III) and our branches in Jangpura and Defence Colony, we prepare case files, paper books and court sets for advocates, law firms and litigants.

Legal copying is our main work. Alongside it, we also offer everyday printing, lamination, fax and stationery at our shops.

See Our Services

Our Mission

To give the legal community fast, accurate and dependable bulk copying, so every filing goes in complete and on time.

Our Approach

Clear copies, correct order and respect for court deadlines are at the heart of how we work.
